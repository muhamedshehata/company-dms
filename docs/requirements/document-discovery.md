## 1. Purpose

Identify the document categories, business purposes, metadata, access needs, and retention requirements for the DMS.

- Finance
- Human Resources (HR)
- Purchasing
- Sales
- Management

## 4. Questions to Resolve

For each document type, determine:

- Who creates or receives it?
- Is it paper, digital, or both?
- Which metadata fields are required?
- Who may view, upload, edit, approve, or delete it?
- Does it require approval or a defined workflow?
- How long must it be retained?
- Does it have a related record in Odoo?
- Does it contain personal, financial, or otherwise restricted information?
- Is OCR and full-text search required?

## 5. Constraints

- Do not upload real company documents to GitHub.
- Use fictional examples when documenting or testing.
- Do not treat example categories as approved business requirements.
- Confirm retention and compliance requirements with the appropriate company owner.

## 8. Initial Discovery Approach

1. Inventory existing paper folders by department and year.
2. Identify existing digital file locations and file types.
3. Record document categories without copying confidential document contents.
4. Identify duplicate, mixed, or unclear categories for review.
5. Determine the metadata and access requirements for each confirmed document category.
6. Select a small representative sample for a controlled scanning and search pilot.

## 9. Items Still to Validate

- Exact document types within each department.
- Whether digital files are already organized consistently.
- Whether paper and digital copies refer to the same documents.
- Required metadata fields for each document type.
- Departmental access permissions and approval workflows.
- Retention requirements and links to Odoo records.
- Whether the estimated 14,500 pages includes all paper archives or only the currently identified collection.

No unverified document categories or business rules are approved by this inventory.
